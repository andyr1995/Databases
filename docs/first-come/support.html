Help and contact

How do I play?

Tap a block and its colour runs out along each of its arrows, claiming every free square until something is in the way. A claimed square stays claimed, so whoever goes later gets cut short. Each block goes once. Fill every square to finish the level: the whole puzzle is the order. Press and hold a block to preview what it would claim.

What is a slip?

A tap that would leave a square that no remaining block can reach. The game undoes it for you, shows which squares would have been stranded, and counts one slip. Finishing with no slips earns three stars.

How does the Daily work?

There is one Daily puzzle per day and it is the same for everyone. You have three hearts and each slip costs one. A new puzzle arrives at midnight, your time.

I bought Remove Ads and adverts are back

Open Settings > Restore purchases in the game while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I change my advert privacy choices?

Open Settings > Privacy choices in the game (shown where the law requires it), or change tracking permission in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move to a new phone by itself.
