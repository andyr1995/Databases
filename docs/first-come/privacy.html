Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

First Come is a puzzle game. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and its developer does not run any servers that receive information about you. This page explains what little data is involved.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, streak, hints and settings are saved on your own device. They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in a browser) deletes them.

Adverts in the app

The iPhone app shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver adverts, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use:

  • a device advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when asked);
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location;
  • information about your device and about how you interact with adverts.

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the app asks for your choices before any advert is requested, and you can change them at any time under Settings > Privacy choices inside the game. On iPhone you can also change tracking permission in the system Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you still get adverts, but they are not personalised.

Google explains how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your name, your payment details or your Apple ID.

The web version

The web version of First Come shows no adverts and sets no cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address when your browser requests the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

First Come is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the support page.
